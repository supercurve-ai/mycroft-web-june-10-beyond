Founded in 2023 by Roy Pereira and Alexey Adamsky**,**Unified is an API and MCP data infrastructure company that enables software companies to securely access their customers’ data across 350+ third-party platforms, including CRMs and HR systems, as well as accounting, messaging, and recruiting tools.

Because Unified handles sensitive, personal information, a security failure could expose not just their customers but their customers' customers, making security and compliance a sink-or-swim issue for their business model.
