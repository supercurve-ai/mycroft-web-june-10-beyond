Security shouldn't slow you down. It should accelerate your business.

Led by Luge Capital, with participation from Brightspark Ventures, Graphite Ventures, and our existing investors Ripple Ventures, Developer Capital, Antler, and BoxOne Ventures, this funding accelerates our mission to empower organizations with enterprise-grade security capabilities—*without*the typical overhead.


## **The problem**

In an ideal world, robust security programs would enable businesses to expand—safely and swiftly. But enterprise-grade security wasn’t built for rapid AI development. Just take a look at your typical security stack:

- Scattered point solutions
- Tedious manual workflows
- Spreadsheet-driven audits
- Checklists upon checklists

Companies stitch together SOC 2 guides, vulnerability scanners, device managers, and access review systems, each demanding its own particular combination of configurations, integrations, and operational debt.


## **The result?**

Instead of driving business, security teams are left drowning in checkbox compliance tasks, forced to operate with patchwork solutions that drain resources, bury critical insights, and stall innovation with bottlenecks.

## Our solution: Meet your AI Security and Compliance Officer

Mycroft enables organizations to replace sprawling, complex security systems with a single platform. Purpose-built for enterprise-grade security and without the usual bloat. Mycroft automates security, maintenance, and compliance functions from start to finish, all in one interface. Our AI Security and Compliance Officer continuously:

- Monitors cloud infrastructure
- Secures devices
- Enforces policies
- Manages incidents
- Prepares companies for audits, including SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA, CMMC, FedRAMP, FedRAMP 20X and more

Think of it as your virtual CISO, GRC expert, and IT/Ops team, rolled into one. Here's what makes us different:

- **All-in-one security and compliance platform:** No more juggling a billion different solutions. Mycroft handles compliance, security, and device management in one place, integrating with over 250 tools to consolidate your entire security and compliance program.
- **Agentic AI support:** Mycroft’s no-code AI agents don't just automate tasks; they adapt to your environment, making intelligent decisions in real-time.
- **Automated security measures for real-world scenarios:** Processes that normally demand hours of manual effort happen automatically in Mycroft. Stolen employee laptop? Our AI agent kicks into gear within seconds: locking the device, flagging compliance workflows, notifying stakeholders, and generating incident reports.
- **Built for modern teams:** Move fast, *without*breaking things. Mycroft was designed to empower companies of every size to scale safely—even (and especially) without a dedicated security team.
